# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Assume the person on the other side is competent and acting in good
faith, and that if something looks wrong to you, you may be the one missing context.

Expected:

- Critique the work, not the person. "This misses the unelevated case" is useful; "this is careless"
  is not.
- Say what you actually did and what you actually saw. Guesses are fine when labelled as guesses.
- Accept a correction without ceremony, and offer one the same way.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or dismissing someone's report without reading it.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Being wrong is normal here

This project's history is largely a record of confident, incorrect beliefs being corrected by
measurement — a Sysinternals build that silently answered nothing, a documented parameter that
described behaviour the code never had, a claim in a comment that Procmon needed a desktop when it
did not. Nobody is expected to be right first time. What is expected is that when the evidence
arrives, the claim changes.

Correcting a maintainer with evidence is a contribution, not a challenge.

## Reporting

Raise conduct concerns with the maintainers at `<contact — fill in>`. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers who do not follow this document are subject to it like everyone else.

For security vulnerabilities, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead — those follow a different
process.
